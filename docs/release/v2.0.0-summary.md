### Summary since v1.0.0

#### Viewing & navigation
- Back and forward restore the exact view after any jump (Back button, Alt+←/→, ⌘[ / ⌘], mouse side buttons).
- Read tools: hand, text selection, magnifier (hold Z); PDF links are keyboard-focusable.
- Outline derived from headings and the table of contents when a PDF has no bookmarks.
- Page labels (i, ii, 1, 2 …) in thumbnails and the page field; page sidebar resizable up to 480 px.

#### Comments & markup
- Word-style comments in a margin column with reply and resolve; Enter saves, Esc discards.
- Comments panel with filter (type, page, status, tag) and sort.
- Comments from Acrobat, Foxit, Okular and other editors appear as editable comments, including rich text and reply threads.
- Stamps (Draft, Approved, Confidential, Received, custom text) as real PDF stamp annotations.
- History panel: jump to any state of the session or delete an annotation from the list.
- Four palettes (Default, Earth, Berry, Study) for all annotation tools; existing annotations never change.

#### Drawing & shapes
- Draw is freehand with strong smoothing and adds a freehand arrow and a freehand shape; it never straightens a stroke.
- Shapes are rigid rectangles, ellipses, lines and arrows with handles, and recognise loosely drawn circles, ellipses, rectangles and lines.

#### Editing text & pages
- Edit text: click a line of the PDF's own text and change it in the document's real font with a live preview. Alignment is kept; "Wrap" re-flows the paragraph where there is room. A bundled substitute font covers missing characters and is named in a notice.
- Surgical redaction: only text, drawings and image areas under the marks are removed; the rest stays searchable text.
- Crop applies straight from the tool.
- Headers and footers: text, page number, date or file name in six positions with a page range.

#### Forms & signatures
- Forms are fillable as soon as a document has fields; "Flatten form" is in File.
- Visual signatures: draw, type or image.
- Certificate signatures (PAdES B-B) with a self-signed or imported .p12/.pfx certificate, written to a new file. Not a qualified electronic signature.
- Signed PDFs are validated on open (signer, time, changes after signing) and locked as their certification allows; "Make editable copy…" writes an unsigned copy.

#### Smart links & sources/citations
- Smart links: footnote markers, table-of-contents lines, references ("see p. 12", "Fig. 3", "§ 5") and literature references ("[12]", "[3–5]") become clickable. They are never written into the PDF and can be switched off.
- Cite: turn a selection into a highlight with margin bubble, page label and short citation, with own coloured tags.
- Source detection reads the imprint and first pages (edition, publisher, ISBN, DOI, document type) into a Reference tab in Document Properties.
- Styles: APA 7 (default), MLA 9, Chicago author-date, DIN ISO 690, German footnote style with bibliography. Copy, or save as .txt, .html, .md, .ris or .bib.
- Export comments, citations and highlights as a PDF summary or Markdown.

#### OCR & export
- Recognize text: scanned pages get an invisible text layer for search, copy, highlighting and smart links, with page choice, progress and Stop. Uses Windows text recognition or Apple Vision; nothing leaves the computer.
- Save as text PDF after recognition: flowing text with headings, optionally followed by the original page images.

#### Home & layout
- New brand "sheer.": Solar Yellow, Inter, light only.
- Home: side navigation, greeting, open documents above recent files, favourites, eight PDF tools. Closing the last document returns to Home.
- Editor: tab strip with undo, redo, history and search; mode card (Read, Comment, Fill & Sign, Pages, Edit; keys 1–5); tool settings in a 300 px inspector; status bar; full menu bar.
- Short tips with clips; Settings can turn them off.

#### Accessibility & languages
- English and German UI.
- Screen-reader headings and named regions, focus returned after dialogs, reduced-motion variants.

#### Privacy & security
- Local only: no accounts, telemetry or cloud services.
- Hostile signed and scanned files are bounded by size and time limits; the final audit found no critical or high issues.

#### Performance & reliability
- Fixed large images rendering half grey and stale annotation lists after deleting a page.
- Recovery of unsaved changes, discarded after 30 days; long text edits and OCR runs honour time limits.
