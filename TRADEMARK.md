## What is not licensed

- The name **"Sheer"** (as a product name for PDF or document software).
- The Sheer logo and its variants (`assets/brand/`) and icons generated from it.

## What this means for forks

You are welcome to fork, modify and redistribute the code under the AGPL. If you distribute a modified version,
you **must rename it** and replace the logo and icons, so that users are not misled about its origin.
Saying that your product is "based on Sheer" is fine.

## Changing the name in a fork

The product name is defined in one central `APP_NAME` token; replace it together with the bundle identifier
(`app.sheer.desktop`) and the files in `assets/brand/`.
